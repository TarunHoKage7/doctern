How it works

Gemma 4 organizes the case and chooses local evidence lookups. MedGemma reviews independently and performs the final specialist review of any contested point. The app controls the sequence, and a fresh Gemma check removes anything unsupported before you see it. It is designed to run fully on this machine; this demo calls Gemma 4 through the online API.
